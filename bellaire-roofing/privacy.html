Privacy policy

Concept demo. This page is a draft for Bellaire Roofing to approve. The demo itself stores and sends nothing.

Bellaire Roofing serves Houston, TX. This page explains what the estimate form collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, the kind of job and any details you type. Nothing is collected until you press Send.

Why

Only to answer your request: to call you back, give an estimate and book the work.

Text messages

We text you only if you tick the box on the form. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or HELP for help. Consent is never required to get an estimate.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. The service that delivers the form to us handles it only for that purpose.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your request as long as we need it to do the job and keep normal business records. To see, correct or delete what we hold, call (713) 667-7458.
