Why one shingle matters

Water follows the courses.

Rain runs from each course onto the one below. Where a shingle has lifted or gone missing, water can get underneath instead. Hold the button to watch it happen.

After hail or wind, look from the ground

  • Shingles or pieces of shingle in the yard
  • Granules collecting at the downspouts
  • Dents in gutters, vents or flashing
  • A new stain on a ceiling or wall
  • Stay off the roof. Ask for an inspection instead.
