What causes a running faucet?

Usually a worn washer, O-ring or cartridge inside the handle. Shut off the supply valves under the sink before opening it up. If the drip continues after replacing the part, the valve seat may be worn and needs a professional.

How do I get hair out of my sink's drain?

Remove the stopper and pull hair out with a plastic drain tool. Avoid chemical drain cleaners, which can damage older pipes. If water still drains slowly, the clog is further down the line and needs cleaning.

My water heater is leaking. What first?

Turn off the water supply to the heater and its power or gas, then call. A leak from the tank itself usually means replacement. Leaks at fittings can often be repaired.

Should I go tankless?

Tankless units give endless hot water and save space, but cost more up front. Professor Plumb installs both tank and tankless and can compare them for your home.
